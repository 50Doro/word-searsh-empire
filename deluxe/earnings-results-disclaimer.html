This is an app, not an income system

Word Search Empire Deluxe is a self-contained application that builds puzzle-book interiors and covers on your own device. It is sold as software, nothing more. It is not a business opportunity, a franchise, an investment, or a “done for you” income system.

No income or results claims

We do not claim, promise or guarantee that you will earn any amount of money, make any number of sales, get any book approved or achieve any particular result. Puzzle counts, page counts, trim sizes and spine measurements shown are descriptions of what the app produces — not a prediction or promise of what you will earn.

What your results depend on

  • the time and effort you put in;
  • your book, your niche and the quality of what you publish;
  • competition, pricing and demand in your market;
  • the rules, review processes and decisions of any platform you use;
  • factors outside anyone’s control.

Because these vary from person to person, results differ. Many people who publish puzzle books do not make money from them.

Review your book before you publish

The app generates puzzles automatically. Please open your book and check the puzzles and answers before you publish or print at volume. The cover generator uses standard spine and bleed measurements as a guide; always check the finished file against the current requirements of your printer before you upload it.
