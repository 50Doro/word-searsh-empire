Standard word searches

Start from one of 24 built-in themes — about 280 words each, nearly 6,700 in all — or paste your own list. The app places every word in one of eight directions, then fills the gaps with random letters. A hidden or shaped style changes only the layout, never the rules.

Hidden messages

Give the app a phrase and it threads the leftover letters through the grid in reading order. The puzzle works exactly as normal — the message is simply waiting in the letters nobody circles.

  • Your phrase, spelled out in the spare cells

Cover generator

A full wrap, measured for you

Give the app a title, a subtitle, an author, a blurb and your page count. It works out the spine from the paper type, adds the bleed around every edge and lays the whole thing out as one print-ready sheet — front, spine and back together.

Thirty-three cover themes, seventeen background motifs and seven title text styles are included, so a shelf of books does not look like one template repeated. A clear rectangle is reserved at the foot of the back cover for the barcode, and the text keeps a safe margin from the trim.

How the spine is worked out

spine (in) = pages × 0.002252 white paper
spine (in) = pages × 0.002500 cream paper
120 pages, white → 0.270 in spine
full wrap = 2 × 8.5 + 0.270 + 2 × 0.125 bleeding
→ 17.520 × 11.250 in

Paper weights and printing practices differ. Always compare the finished file against the current template for your printer, trim size and page count before you upload, and adjust the page count in the app if the printer asks for it.

What Deluxe is — and what it is not

Deluxe is a fast, private tool for making puzzle books. It saves you time on the fiddly parts: placing words, laying out pages, sizing a cover, checking answers. That is all it does, and it does it well.

To be straight with you, it does not:

  • upload anything to Amazon, KDP, Etsy or any other shop — you publish by hand;
  • write your listing, choose your niche or design your brand for you;
  • promise sales, rankings, reviews, approvals or any particular result;
  • need an account, a subscription, an internet connection or an AI key;
  • send anything you type anywhere. The file runs in your browser and stays there.

People who make puzzle books sell very different numbers of them. Some sell none. What you build and how you market it is entirely up to you. This is a tool, not a business in a box.

Do I need the main app for Deluxe to work?
No. Deluxe is a complete, standalone file with its own theme library. It is sold as an upgrade because it builds on the same idea, and it sits happily alongside the books you already make — but it does not need anything else to run.
Does it need an internet connection?
No. Once the file is on your device it opens and runs with no network at all. There is nothing to sign into and nothing to activate.
Do I need an AI subscription or an API key?
No. Every puzzle is generated by the app itself, on your computer. There is no AI service involved and no key to enter.
Is anything I type sent anywhere?
No. The page makes no third-party requests. Your themes, titles, blurbs and word lists stay in your browser.
Will this help me sell books?
It can save you time making books and writing a listing, which is part of the work. It cannot promise sales, rankings, reviews or approvals, and it will not upload anything to a shop for you. How well a book does depends on the market, the cover, the listing and your own effort — none of which we control.

A print-on-demand account (such as KDP) or a printer, a shop or marketplace account, and the time to write your listing and upload your files. Deluxe gives you the interior and the cover; you supply the rest. It is not affiliated with Amazon, Kindle Direct Publishing or Etsy.
Which page sizes are supported?
Letter (8.5 × 11 in), A4, 6 × 9 in, 5.5 × 8.5 in and 8.5 × 8.5 in square. The cover generator uses the same trim you choose, plus the page count, to work out the spine.
